      Assessments & diagnostics

      PILOT

      See how your people work, and where to grow them.

      The HUMAP Behavioral Competency Assessment is built for Filipino workplaces. It combines a work-style self-report, situational judgment, behavioral evidence and observer input.

      What it looks at

      Four areas of workplace behavior.

      Working Well with OthersCollaboration, respect and teamwork across levels.
      Drive to Master the JobLearning, ownership and quality of work.
      Making Things HappenInitiative, follow-through and results.
      Preparing Others to LeadCoaching, delegation and developing successors.
      IMPORTANT

      While in pilot, results are for development conversations only. They are not used to hire, reject or promote anyone until the assessment has been validated. Group reports hide small groups to protect individuals.
